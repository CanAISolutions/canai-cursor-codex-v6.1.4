## Inference Engine Validation

### Business Name Extraction Patterns
- Pattern 1: `/for\s+([A-Z][A-Za-z0-9\s&]+)/` - "Build AI for TechCo"
- Pattern 2: `/help\s+([A-Z][A-Za-z0-9\s&]+)\s+with/i` - "Help DataCorp with automation"
- Fallback: Generate from solution type or default to "Your Business"

### Brand Voice Mapping
- "technical": ML, analytics, API, data science contexts
- "innovative": AI, automation, chatbot contexts  
- "professional": healthcare, finance, enterprise contexts
- "approachable": customer support, user-facing contexts
- "strategic": business, management, leadership contexts
- "authoritative": executive, C-suite, decision-maker contexts

### Success Metrics Templates
- Analytics: "30d: Data pipeline; 60d: Forecast model; 90d: 15% accuracy improvement"
- Chatbot: "30d: Prototype deployment; 60d: 100 conversations; 90d: 50% ticket automation"
- Healthcare: "30d: Algorithm training; 60d: Clinical validation; 90d: FDA submission"
- Default: "30d: Prototype; 60d: Beta; 90d: Launch"

### Linked Prompts Logic
- Always include: "business-plan"
- Chatbot/Support: + "email-campaign"
- Analytics/Business: + "ad-amplify"
- Website/Web: + "site-audit"
- Social context: + "social-content"
