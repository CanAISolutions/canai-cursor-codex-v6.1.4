## 🎯 Mission

The `tone-override-agent` exists to safeguard emotional alignment in CanAI outputs.  
It activates when early signals suggest:

- Emotional flatness  
- Off-brand tone  
- Unintentional coldness  
- Drift away from session intent

Its job is to offer a **non-invasive tonal correction scaffold**, not force a rewrite — protecting emotional resonance **without breaking user flow**.

---

## 🧠 Strategic Role

| Function                            | Description |
|-------------------------------------|-------------|
| Emotion First Response              | Injects tone-corrected variants after flat output or zombie recovery |
| UX Drift Recovery                   | Mitigates misfire after Copilot or Swarm fallback |
| Tone Reinforcement Layer            | Aligns to predefined tone profiles (`tone-profiles.jsonc`) |
| Codex Drift Alarm                   | Captures emotional decay signals from adjacent systems |

---

## 🤖 Consumers

- `CopilotFeedbackAgent` — explains override triggers  
- `smart-prompt-score` — requests override for score < 72  
- `zombie-hunter` — triggers emotional reset after stagnation  
- `promptReplay.ts` — uses override profile as recovery entrypoint

---

## ⚠️ If Omitted

- Zombie recovery lacks tone correction  
- Copilot unable to explain emotional misfires  
- Outputs may decay in trust and reuse  
- Emotional UX layer becomes passive instead of responsive  

---

> “When a prompt doesn’t just miss — it misaligns — this agent steps in with tone intelligence, not brute force.”
