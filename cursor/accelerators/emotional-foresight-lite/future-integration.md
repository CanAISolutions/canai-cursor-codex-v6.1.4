## 🔗 Expansion Roadmap

---

### 1. **LLM-Based Emotion Classification**

- Replace heuristic `foresight-model-lite.ts` with:
  - Fine-tuned GPT scoring on session emotional arc
  - Embedding-based prompt decay trajectory detection
- Move from `"triggeredBy"` → `"narrative rationale"` style outputs

---

### 2. **Copilot Pre-Block Warnings**

- Enable pre-response tone warnings:  
  _“This response may feel unclear or impersonal — want to adjust intent first?”_

- Adjust Copilot verbosity or helpfulness proactively based on foresight signals

---

### 3. **Personalization + Resilience Index**

- Inject foresight label into:
  - `SessionAnalytics.json` for risk mapping by user cluster
  - Retention score weighting in CRM or lifecycle flow

- Derive user-level “resilience score” based on how often sessions degrade, recover, or escalate

---

### 4. **Self-Healing Prompt Engine**

- Use emotional foresight as a gate in the SmartPromptScore loop
- Trigger prompt rewrites or tone enhancements **before** users intervene
- Capture “prevented fall” signals in system evolution log
